- Parse the target industry, optional `--location`, and optional `--depth`.
- Execute the 7 specialized agents:
  1. Market Intelligence Agent
  2. Competitor Intelligence Agent
  3. Customer Intelligence Agent
  4. Business Opportunity Agent
  5. High-Value Prospect Researcher (Zero data fabrication)
  6. Marketing Strategist Agent
  7. Business Strategy & Action Plan Agent
- Consult `SKILL.md` and `skills/` for the 21 modular sub-skills.
